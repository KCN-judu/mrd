# AN19 fixed-snapshot event-cardinality bound

## Scope

This note proves the number of semantic events and queue items generated by
`An19ReducedEventEngine` for one fixed projection snapshot. It also certifies a
conservative exact-comparison bound for the engine's practical stable binary
heap. It does not prove the source-equivalent `O(m+n log log n)` event-order
structure, bound exact-rational bit complexity, or amortize events across
recursive snapshots.

Let `n` be the number of vertices in the snapshot's `remaining` set and let
`m` be the number of edges in the supplied graph. Using the full graph edge
count is conservative because the reduced adjacency contains only edges whose
two endpoints are in `remaining`.

## Semantic events

The canonical trace has these bounds:

1. At most `n` vertex-entry events: the threshold array has one slot per
   remaining vertex and emits at most one entry for each populated slot.
2. At most `n` highway-endpoint events: one can follow only a nonstale vertex
   entry.
3. At most `n` stopping-condition checks: one is emitted per distinct populated
   threshold radius, and there are at most `n` populated thresholds.
4. At most `2m` directed-incidence transitions: each undirected edge follows
   the monotone state sequence outside, boundary, internal and therefore
   changes state at most twice.
5. At most `2m` virtual-segment events: one is emitted only as provenance for a
   transition on a virtual segment.
6. At most two structural events: one portal-split event and one contraction
   event.

Consequently the complete semantic trace has at most

```text
3n + 4m + 2
```

records. This includes vertex entries discarded after the selected stopping
radius.

## Queue items

The reduced engine initially inserts the target and vertices on the recovered
simple path. An interior portal can attempt at most two additional endpoint
seeds, so there are at most `n + 2` seed insertions. `add_trace_seed` inserts
only a strict label improvement.

After a vertex is settled, its adjacency is never scanned again. Each active
undirected edge contributes at most two directed arcs, each directed arc is
examined at most once from its settled tail, and one examination can produce at
most one strict-improvement insertion. Relaxations therefore add at most `2m`
items. The total insertion bound is

```text
n + 2m + 2.
```

The loop drains the queue, so the pop count equals the insertion count. Stale
items are a subset of popped items and maximum queue occupancy is no larger
than total insertions.

## Machine certificate

Every `An19EventRun` stores an `An19LocalEventBoundCertificate`. Trace
verification reconstructs every event category, the queue insertion/pop/stale
counts, both formulas, and their agreement with snapshot metrics. It rejects a
changed formula, count, dimension, or an attempted priority-queue proof claim.
The A--H evidence in `results/an19-event-adversarial.json` contains verified
certificates for both the exact Oracle and reduced engine in all 31 cases.

## Practical exact heap bound

The reduced engine inserts every seed and strict relaxation improvement into a
stable binary min-heap ordered by exact distance, vertex ID, and insertion
sequence. Let `I` be the total number of insertions and let
`L = ceil(log2(max(I, 1)))`. Each push performs at most `L` item comparisons,
and each pop performs at most two comparisons per level, hence at most `2L`.
Because every settled vertex scans its adjacency once, relaxation compares a
candidate with an existing label at most once per directed arc, hence at most
`2m` times. The total counted comparisons are therefore at most

```text
I L + 2 I L + 2m = 3 I L + 2m.
```

`An19PracticalQueueBoundCertificate` stores the three observed comparison
classes and the three reconstructed bounds. It exists only on reduced-engine
runs; the independent Oracle retains its explicit sort and has no practical
heap certificate. Verification rejects changed formulas, strategy, proof
scope, counts, or an attempted AN19-target claim. In the bounded A--H campaign,
the maximum observed total is 473 and the maximum conservative bound is 1,112.

## Remaining proof boundary

The binary heap replaces the earlier linear minimum scan and proves a practical
`O((n+m) log(n+m))` comparison bound per fixed snapshot. That is asymptotically
weaker than the required source-equivalent `O(m+n log log n)` ordering bound.
This note therefore establishes `local_event_bound_proved = true` while
retaining all of the following as false:

- `global_amortization_proved`
- `priority_queue_bound_proved`
- `an19_runtime_verified`

The production hierarchy remains in `ReducedLengthMonotone` and
`AggregateRegressionOnly` modes, and `source_runtime_verified()` remains
false.
